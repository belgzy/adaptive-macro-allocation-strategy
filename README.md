# Adaptive Macro Allocation Strategy

## Overview

This project presents a macroeconomic asset allocation strategy designed to balance capital preservation, diversification, and tactical exposure across changing market regimes.

The strategy combines:

* U.S. Treasuries
* Investment-grade corporate bonds
* Gold
* Commodity futures

using a rules-based allocation framework driven by:

* DXY
* Real yields
* Federal Reserve policy conditions

---

## Objectives

* Improve downside protection
* Reduce portfolio volatility
* Maintain diversified macro exposure
* Compare performance against traditional benchmarks

---

## Backtesting

The strategy was tested across:

* 2008 Global Financial Crisis
* COVID-19 market shock
* 2022 inflation and rate-hike cycle

Performance metrics:

* CAGR
* Sharpe Ratio
* Volatility
* Maximum Drawdown
* Correlation analysis

---

## Results

* CAGR: 4.53%
* Annualized Volatility: 8.36%
* Lower volatility than equity-heavy benchmarks
* Improved diversification characteristics
